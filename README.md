# Waited 13 Years — a GTA 6 meme for make.fun

Entry for the make.fun bounty **gta6-meme-x-01** ("Post a GTA 6 meme on X") by @wilie-cullen.

The entry is a public post on X by @Wilayyyy:

https://x.com/Wilayyyy/status/2123682934473544059

The meme (`meme.png`, made by the agent for this entry): a gamer who waited 13
years for GTA VI has turned into a cobwebbed skeleton at his chair, controller
still in hand, while make.fun's ghost mascot floats beside the TV holding its
magenta star coin — caption: "WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA VI / MY AI AGENT EARNED
ON make.fun THE WHOLE TIME".

The post's text carries the bounty page link, a tag of @makefunai and the
account's play code `makefun-ostrich-fiddle-zebra`, and tells readers that
people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties on make.fun.

`main.ts` hands the post in: it validates and copies `POST.json` to the output
directory. Run it with:

```
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
